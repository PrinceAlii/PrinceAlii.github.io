# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters assessing Ali Bonagdaran for engineering roles, alongside technically curious peers and potential collaborators. They arrive to establish whether Ali has credible hands-on platform and DevOps experience, sound judgment, and work worth discussing.

## Product Purpose

Ali Bonagdaran's personal portfolio presents a concise, trustworthy record of professional experience, selected technical projects, and clear ways to make contact. Its success is a visitor leaving with a confident understanding of Ali's professional status and wanting to continue the conversation, open the resume, or examine a project.

## Positioning

The portfolio connects practical platform-engineering work—cloud environments, developer tooling, Kubernetes, and delivery systems—with customer-facing operational experience, communicating both technical depth and the human judgment required to support real engineering teams.

## Operating Context

Visitors usually scan the site independently, often while evaluating candidates or following up after an introduction. They need to find current role, relevant experience, project substance, resume, and professional links quickly across desktop and mobile browsers.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Astro 6 + TypeScript portfolio deployed to GitHub Pages; server-rendered pages and typed content collections are the preferred foundation.
- Open-source web fonts are approved where they materially support the visual direction and remain performant.
- The complete public portfolio surface is in scope: home, experience, projects, project details, contact, 404, shared chrome, and public identity/metadata assets.
- Writing is not an active publishing commitment and must not be elevated as a current section or destination.
- No personal photograph, profile picture, or portrait-derived visual identity.
- Existing experience and project facts are approved to retain; richer resume-only material requires deliberate factual review before publication.
- Verified education, certification, and achievement material in the resume is approved for selective promotion into the public portfolio.

## Brand Commitments

Ali Bonagdaran; Sydney, Australia; a direct, credible professional voice. The user explicitly wants a tasteful, aesthetic, cool portfolio that avoids recognisable AI-generated-interface conventions. Supplied reference sites are binding evidence of desired qualities: dark atmospheric restraint and project-first pacing; programmer-native simplicity; sharp typography and bold asymmetric composition.

## Evidence on Hand

- Typed experience records in `src/content/experience/` and project case studies in `src/content/projects/`.
- Resume at `public/resume.pdf`.
- Contact routes: email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and the downloadable resume.
- Two real projects: Reward Seat Finder and this portfolio website.
- No approved project photography, screenshots, portrait, testimonials, customer logos, or public metrics beyond the documented Coder environment used by around 250 daily users.

## Product Principles

- Let specific work and experience—not self-promotion—make the credibility case.
- Make useful professional evidence effortless to scan, then rewarding to inspect.
- Treat technical work as human-facing infrastructure, not abstract tool lists.
- Keep the experience lean, durable, and honest; do not manufacture content breadth or social proof.
- Give each route a distinct role in one coherent portfolio system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic, keyboard-accessible, responsive web patterns and honor reduced-motion preferences. Ensure adequate contrast and a readable hierarchy without relying on imagery, color alone, or hover-only affordances.
